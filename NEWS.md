# News used — Tuesday, 24 November 2026

All stories below happened, or were first reported, on 24 November 2026.
Each was cross-checked in at least two news sources on the day.

## Lead — Strait of Hormuz

- Iran says four tankers hit naval mines in the Strait of Hormuz and caught fire (three first, then a fourth).
- The Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps says its navy targeted a container ship in the strait.
- Iran rules out talks with the US until Washington meets seven conditions; Qatar says diplomacy is ongoing.
- The US administration is debating whether to target senior Iranian leaders (NBC).

## Lead cards

1. **Houthi drone hits Riyadh tower (SAUDI ARABIA).** A drone strike hits a tower in Riyadh's financial district, injuring 11 — the second attack in under a week. The UK sanctions the Houthi group and four officials.
2. **Huawei guilty (UNITED STATES).** A US jury finds Huawei guilty of conspiracy to steal trade secrets and bank fraud, and acquits it of racketeering.
3. **Huang returns as Nvidia chief (MARKETS).** Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO after a call from President Trump; shares rise about 32%, adding roughly $1.5 trillion in market value.

## Small cards

1. **Ukraine strikes Russian ships (UKRAINE).** Ukraine acknowledges a drone strike on the Russian cargo ship Sparta II in the Gulf of Finland, and another vessel is hit off Libya. A Russian strike on Zaporizhzhia kills two and wounds more than 40.
2. **Gaza strike (GAZA).** Israeli airstrikes in Gaza kill seven people, including Hamas's director of internal security, despite the ceasefire in place since last year.
3. **Senegal regain Africa Cup title (SPORT).** Senegal regain the Africa Cup of Nations title after a sports court appeal.
4. **Pakistan's first astronaut (SPACE).** Pakistan Air Force pilot Muhammad Zishan Ali will fly to China's Tiangong space station on the Shenzhou-24 mission, for about a week.

## Also on 24 November

- **Türkiye:** a Turkish Air Force AS-532 helicopter crashes after taking off from Eskisehir for training, killing five crew.
- **Colombia:** armed men seize a supermarket in Cartagena and hold 25 people hostage.
- **Nepal:** a 5.6-magnitude earthquake strikes near the Chinese border.
- **Vietnam:** a gas explosion at a Hanoi restaurant kills six and injures ten.
- **Rohingya:** fourteen countries recognise the International Court of Justice decision that Myanmar committed genocide against the Rohingya.
- **United Nations:** Secretary-General António Guterres calls for a multilateral framework to manage AI risks within a year.
